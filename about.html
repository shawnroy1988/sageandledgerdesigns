About

A quiet shop for practical files.

Sage & Ledger Designs is an Etsy shop of digital downloads for money, time, and home.

The collection covers printable planners, Excel and Google Sheets templates, Power BI dashboard kits, wall art, party printables, and small business files. The palette stays in sage, sand, and terracotta.

This site is a catalog. The Etsy listing is where you buy, and where the file details for that item live.
